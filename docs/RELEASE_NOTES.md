# v1.0.0 — Final 2D research workflow

Released 2026-09-10 as a public-sharing companion to the accepted optical atlas.

Included: 2D country/stage map, selected-company links, supply-focus product cards, supplier/customer matrix, indirect discovery, market-cap area controls, preserved product and future views, conditional conflict controls, English/Simplified Chinese, portable standard-library build, reusable skill and prompt library.

Public-copy differences: removed 1,611 embedded source-image labels, 1,598 unverified imported relationships and non-profile imported company labels. The remaining 40 profiles and 11 reviewed relationships are retained. Original accepted HTML was not overwritten.

Tests: Python data/build integrity; Node behavior checks for directed panels, evidence filtering, all country pairs, optional intermediaries, scenario matching/reset, indirect paths, market-cap dates/area, non-overlapping cards and language state. Browser visual QA was not run.

Research gaps: only 6 sourced direct supply records (5 company-confirmed, 1 third-party); no verified dependency percentages; incomplete factory/shipment geography; one absent and two off-date market-cap values; no independently populated ownership/financing layer; no live market or filing refresh.

Skill validation: frontmatter/resource validation and a portable scaffold/build check. This is not a claim that a complete second-industry research study has been executed.
