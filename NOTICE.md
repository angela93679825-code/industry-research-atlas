# Attribution and public-data scope

The MIT license applies to original application code, build/test helpers and workflow documentation. It does not relicense third-party market data, company filings, press releases, trademarks, images or linked content. Those remain subject to their owners' terms.

Company relationship/product evidence is linked in `data/atlas.json`, with date, status and scope where recorded. Market-cap snapshots link to Stock Analysis company pages; the page's own underlying provider and update terms apply. This repository contains rounded research snapshots and original summaries, not a licensed live data feed or an offer of commercial data redistribution rights.

The supplied private atlas included third-party source-map screenshots and unverified extracted company labels. They are omitted from this public edition and from the portable skill assets. The accepted private original remains separate. Do not add source screenshots, paid reports, proprietary exports, credentials or private customer data to public contributions without the applicable rights.

The data is incomplete and dated. Neither a missing record nor an empty matrix cell proves absence of a business relationship. Scenario outputs are conditional exposure descriptions, not trading recommendations, forecasts of losses or confirmations of actual sanctions.
