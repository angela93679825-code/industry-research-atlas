# Research method

## Intake and identity

Record the task cutoff date, universe-selection rationale, uploaded documents and accessible source pages. Resolve subsidiaries, renamed entities, acquisitions, share classes and ADRs before merging names. A ticker exchange does not establish headquarters. Retain unresolved identities separately.

Extract image/PDF labels with page or image identifiers. OCR confidence is not commercial evidence. If supplied source text is truncated, preserve uncertainty. Keep a provenance ledger of confirmed, reported, inferred, and unresolved claims.

## Industrial map

Start with the physical or economic process and the product bill of materials. Define stage boundaries around what firms provide and who purchases it. Include service, integration and manufacturing roles that do not fit a simple linear sequence. Country layouts may show one primary position, but a firm's profile lists all roles.

The optical reference's process is DSP → driver → modulator → optical waveguide → coupling interface → optical fiber → optical detector → TIA → DSP → receiver, with a laser feeding the transmitter. This is an optical-specific teaching example, not a universal industry process. Distinguish electrical processing, optical conversion and product packaging. For electricity, healthcare, materials or other sectors, establish the appropriate process independently.

## Product record

For each node write three connected explanations:
1. Use, importance and industry position.
2. Present function, commercial product examples and capability (include model, date and units where verified).
3. Potential improvement, the specific changing part/process, technical bottleneck, evidence, and company business implications.

Separate architectures that coexist from inevitable replacement sequences. For example, a packaging change is not automatically removal of all DSP functionality; functions may migrate. Cite vendor material for exact performance claims. Label generic 3D geometry and invented layout as illustrative, never as manufacturer CAD.

## Filing collection

Search official investor relations and securities-regulator repositories. Read the filing; do not rely on search snippets. Capture company, form, filing/publication date, period end, source link, supporting claim and limitations. “Latest” means latest available at the stated cutoff, not the fiscal quarter named in the user's screenshot. If unavailable, state what you did find. A foreign issuer need not file a 10-Q.

Research priorities: product and segment descriptions, customer concentration, material suppliers, geography, manufacturing partners, commitments, capital expenditure, capacity constraints, dependency risks and product-transition disclosures. Do not identify an anonymous “Customer A” without independent support.

## Evidence hierarchy

Company/regulator confirmation, credible third-party reporting and analyst inference must remain distinguishable. Official announcements can establish a design-in or future agreement without proving shipments have begun. Capture that scope in the record note.

Source URL and date belong to the claim, not just the company homepage. Undated evidence is marked undated and excluded from the default dated confirmed-supply filter until its chronology is resolved. Record review date separately.

## Interpretation

A small specialized supplier may be strategically important without being a large company. Assess disclosed customer revenue share, procurement share, sole-source inputs, qualification requirements and documented alternatives separately. Never infer these from map degree or market cap. A connectivity score, if specifically wanted, must publish its formula and evidence sensitivity and must not be labeled a financial dependency score.
